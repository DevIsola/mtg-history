## The Beginning of a Rivalry

Long before the events of many of Magic's best-known stories,
Dominaria was home to two brothers whose rivalry would
reshape the world: Urza and Mishra.

Both possessed extraordinary knowledge of ancient artifacts,
but their competing ambitions eventually drove them into
a devastating conflict.

## A World at War

What began as a personal rivalry grew into a war involving
powerful armies, artificers, and ancient machines.

The consequences of the conflict would extend far beyond
the brothers themselves, affecting the history of Dominaria
for generations.

## A Story Told Across Expansions

The Brothers' War occupies an unusual place in Magic's
publication history.

The _Antiquities_ expansion, released in 1994, introduced
many elements of the conflict through cards and flavor text.

Nearly three decades later, _The Brothers' War_ (2022)
returned to the same historical period, depicting characters
and events through a modern expansion.

This makes the conflict an ideal starting point for
Chronicles of the Multiverse.
