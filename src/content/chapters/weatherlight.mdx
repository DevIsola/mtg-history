## I. The War That Never Ended

The Brothers' War ended with a catastrophe. The Ice Age
eventually passed. Kingdoms rose, fell, and gave way to
new civilizations.

But one conflict remained unfinished.

Urza had survived the destruction of Argoth and become
a planeswalker. Over the centuries that followed, he
dedicated himself to confronting Phyrexia.

The artificial plane ruled by Yawgmoth had already
influenced his brother Mishra and helped turn a
family rivalry into a world-changing war.

Urza understood that Phyrexia remained a danger to
Dominaria, even when its armies could not be seen.

Yet fighting Phyrexia would require more than powerful
spells or a conventional army.

Yawgmoth's forces could travel between planes,
corrupt living beings, and develop machines
designed for conquest.

Urza's answer would take centuries to prepare.

## II. The Legacy and the Skyship

At the center of Urza's plans was the Legacy:
a collection of artifacts and living participants
intended to become a weapon against Phyrexia.

The Legacy was not a single object that could
simply be discovered and activated.

Its components were scattered, and their ultimate
purpose would only become clear when they were
brought together.

Among its most important elements was <CharacterLink id="karn">Karn</CharacterLink>,
a silver golem created by Urza.

Unlike many of the destructive machines built
throughout Dominaria's history, Karn developed
a gentle nature and a reluctance to harm others.

The Weatherlight was another essential part
of the plan.

<HistoricalMoment card={frontmatter.cards[0]}>

The Weatherlight was a remarkable flying ship
capable of traveling between planes.

Its mission would bring its crew into contact
with people, artifacts, and dangers across
the Multiverse.

The ship was both a means of exploration and
a vessel through which pieces of the Legacy
could eventually be reunited.

</HistoricalMoment>

What appeared to be a series of adventures
was connected to a much older design.

The fate of the ship and the destiny of its
crew would become inseparable.

## III. Sisay and the Gathering of the Crew

<CharacterLink id="sisay">Captain Sisay</CharacterLink> commanded the
Weatherlight during its search for the scattered
pieces of the Legacy.

Her crew included individuals with very different
backgrounds and loyalties.

<CharacterLink id="hanna">Hanna</CharacterLink> served as the
ship's navigator and an expert in magical artifacts,
while Orim provided healing and medical knowledge.

Hanna had studied at Tolaria and was the daughter of
Barrin, Urza's trusted colleague. Her decision to pursue
artifice rather than follow her father's preferred path
reflected her independent judgment.

She also shared a complicated history with Gerrard:
they had been close before he abandoned the ship and
the destiny Sisay hoped he would embrace.

Tahngarth, the minotaur warrior, brought
formidable strength to the ship.

Squee, a goblin, became one of its most
unusual and enduring companions.

Karn represented both the crew's capacity
for compassion and the deeper mystery
of the Legacy.

<HistoricalMoment card={frontmatter.cards[1]}>

Sisay understood that the artifacts carried
and sought by the Weatherlight were important.

Her leadership held together a crew whose
personal ambitions did not always align
with its greater mission.

When Sisay was captured by agents of Volrath,
the search for the Legacy became an immediate
rescue operation.

</HistoricalMoment>

Sisay was more than a captain carrying orders. Her faith
in Gerrard's eventual return helped preserve the crew's
purpose while he resisted the Legacy's demands.

<HistoricalMoment card={frontmatter.cards[5]}>

Hanna's technical expertise kept the Weatherlight and
its irreplaceable artifacts functioning during an
extraordinary journey. Her knowledge of machinery and
artifice brought practical insight to decisions that
others approached as questions of duty or destiny.

</HistoricalMoment>

The crew sought <CharacterLink id="gerrard">Gerrard Capashen</CharacterLink>, a warrior
whose life was bound to the Legacy's purpose.

Gerrard had previously left the ship after
personal tragedy.

The danger to Sisay drew him back toward
a destiny he had tried to avoid.

## IV. Rath and the Shadow of Volrath

The rescue mission led the Weatherlight
to Rath, an artificial plane deeply
connected to Phyrexia.

Rath was governed by <CharacterLink id="volrath">Volrath</CharacterLink>,
a shapeshifter whose service to Phyrexia made him
one of the Weatherlight crew's most formidable enemies.

Before becoming the evincar of Rath, Volrath was known
as Vuel. He had grown up alongside Gerrard under Sidar
Kondo's care, but jealousy and resentment turned their
bond into an enduring rivalry.

Sisay's kidnapping thus drew Gerrard into a struggle
with someone from his own past, not merely the ruler
of a distant enemy plane.

<HistoricalMoment card={frontmatter.cards[2]}>

As Rath's evincar, Volrath controlled
the Stronghold and commanded forces
that threatened the Weatherlight crew.

His ability to assume other identities
made deception one of his most dangerous
weapons.

Rath itself was part of a larger strategy:
the artificial plane was being prepared
to help Phyrexia invade Dominaria.

</HistoricalMoment>

The Weatherlight's arrival brought the
crew into conflict with Volrath's servants,
including the fearsome Greven il-Vec.

Separated and pursued, the companions
had to rely on allies within Rath.

Their rescue mission exposed them to a
world whose existence had been shaped
by Yawgmoth's ambitions.

## V. The Stronghold and the Cost of Escape


The Weatherlight's effort to rescue Sisay took the crew
into the dangerous fortress of <CharacterLink id="volrath">Volrath</CharacterLink>.
But the Stronghold held more than military threats.
It also brought a supernatural curse to its terrible conclusion.

<CharacterLink id="crovax">Crovax</CharacterLink>, a nobleman who
had joined the voyage, was searching for the angel Selenia.
He had once freed her from a cursed artifact, only to lose her
when Volrath drew her into his service on Rath.

<HistoricalMoment card={frontmatter.cards[10]}>

Selenia was the angel at the center of Crovax's tragedy.
Within the Stronghold, she confronted the Weatherlight crew.
The encounter forced Crovax to choose between saving a
companion and sparing the angel he loved.

</HistoricalMoment>

When Selenia attacked, <CharacterLink id="mirri">Mirri</CharacterLink>
intervened and suffered severe injuries.
Crovax killed Selenia to save the wounded cat warrior.
His act fulfilled the curse and transformed him into a vampire.

<HistoricalMoment card={frontmatter.cards[7]}>

_Crovax the Cursed_ presents a former companion in the
form created by Selenia's death. His vampirism was not an
innate condition when he first sailed with the Weatherlight:
it was the consequence of a specific tragedy on Rath.

</HistoricalMoment>

<HistoricalEventReference id="selenia-curse-crovax" />

Mirri had fought beside Gerrard since their early years.
She had been wounded while protecting the crew, but her
loyalty and concern for Crovax did not disappear.

After Crovax departed from the Weatherlight, Mirri followed
him. The cursed former companion killed her, ending a
friendship that had once helped hold the ship's crew together.

<HistoricalMoment card={frontmatter.cards[8]}>

_Mirri, Cat Warrior_ preserves the original version of the
Weatherlight fighter in _Exodus_. Her death was part of the
established story, not the alternative fate later explored
in _Planar Chaos_.

</HistoricalMoment>

<HistoricalEventReference id="mirris-final-confrontation" />

The rescue mission also depended on
<CharacterLink id="ertai">Ertai</CharacterLink>, a gifted young
wizard trained in Tolaria. The Predator had damaged the
Weatherlight's planeshifting system, forcing the crew to
seek another route away from Rath.

Ertai worked to open the escape portal. When the crew was
delayed at the Stronghold, the ship had to use the Legacy's
Skyshaper to reach the portal before it closed.
The Weatherlight escaped, but Ertai was left behind.

<HistoricalMoment card={frontmatter.cards[9]}>

_Ertai, Wizard Adept_ depicts the capable but boastful mage
before his later transformation. His abandonment on Rath
was one of the voyage's most consequential losses: the
wizard who made the escape possible could not join it.

</HistoricalMoment>

<HistoricalEventReference id="ertai-stranded-on-rath" />

Sisay was rescued, but the crew did not leave intact.
Mirri was dead, Crovax had broken from his companions,
and Ertai remained stranded on an enemy plane.
The struggle against Phyrexia had become intensely personal.

## VI. Mercadia and the Hidden Fleet

The Weatherlight's escape from Rath
carried the surviving crew to Mercadia.

This unfamiliar plane presented challenges
very different from those of Volrath's
Stronghold.

Trade, political maneuvering, and conflicting
local interests complicated the crew's
efforts to recover and repair their ship.

Volrath's influence also followed them.

Disguised as Takara, Starke's daughter, Volrath
had accompanied the rescued crew off Rath. The
shapeshifter used the disguise to sow distrust,
concealing his true identity until events on
Mercadia exposed him.

<HistoricalMoment card={frontmatter.cards[6]}>

_Unmask_ depicts Volrath emerging from Takara's
appearance. The card captures one of the saga's
most consequential acts of deception: a Phyrexian
agent moving among the heroes he had tormented.

</HistoricalMoment>

Volrath murdered Starke while still concealed
among the companions, showing how little safety
the Weatherlight's escape from Rath had actually
provided.

<HistoricalEventReference id="volrath-unmasked" />

More importantly, the crew discovered that
Mercadia had become another part of the
Phyrexian invasion preparations.

A hidden fleet of warships was being built
beneath the great inverted mountain
of Mercadia City.

The threat was no longer limited to Rath.

Phyrexia had established preparations
across multiple worlds.

The Weatherlight eventually escaped
Mercadia and returned toward Dominaria
with the knowledge that an invasion
was approaching.

Events on Rath continued while the Weatherlight pursued its
mission across other planes. Crovax's transformation had
separated him from his former companions, and his ambitions
turned toward the leadership of the Phyrexian-controlled world.

When Volrath returned from Mercadia, he and Crovax fought
for the office of evincar. With Ertai's assistance, Crovax
killed his former master and took his place as ruler of Rath.

<HistoricalMoment card={frontmatter.cards[11]}>

_Ascendant Evincar_ depicts Crovax after his victory over
Volrath. The card does not portray the original curse, but
a later phase of the same character's descent into Phyrexian
power.

</HistoricalMoment>

Ertai's story also continued in a darker direction. His
experiences on Rath exposed him to Phyrexian transformation,
and the young wizard who had saved the Weatherlight became an
agent of those he had once resisted.

<HistoricalMoment card={frontmatter.cards[12]}>

_Ertai, the Corrupted_ contrasts with _Ertai, Wizard Adept_:
two printings that capture the same person at very different
points in the Weatherlight Saga.

</HistoricalMoment>

## VII. Urza's Long Design Revealed

The story of the Weatherlight cannot be
understood without returning to Urza's
earlier preparations.

Across the centuries, Urza had pursued
knowledge, powerful artifacts, and
allies capable of resisting Phyrexia.

His work involved the Tolarian Academy,
the creation of Karn, and long-term
plans that would affect future generations.

These preparations were extensive,
but they were not without moral cost.

Urza's determination to defeat Yawgmoth
frequently led him to treat other lives
as parts of a larger design.

Gerrard's place in the Legacy was
therefore more than coincidence.

The Weatherlight crew carried the
consequences of decisions made long
before many of them were born.

Much of this earlier history would
be revealed to Magic readers through
stories published after the first
Weatherlight adventures.

The difference between the publication
order and the fictional chronology
is essential to understanding the saga.

## VIII. The Invasion of Dominaria

Phyrexia eventually moved from
preparation to open war.

Portals brought invading forces
to Dominaria, and entire regions
faced devastation.

Benalia was among the lands attacked
during the opening stages.

The defenders of Dominaria were
forced to cooperate against a
threat greater than their own
political rivalries.

Urza had spent centuries preparing
for this confrontation.

Yet even his plans could not
prevent widespread destruction.

<HistoricalMoment card={frontmatter.cards[3]}>

Gerrard became one of the central
figures in the effort to resist
the Phyrexian invasion.

His struggle involved not only
battlefield leadership but also
difficult choices concerning
his companions and the Legacy.

The war brought devastating losses
to the Weatherlight crew. Hanna,
its navigator and artificer, succumbed
to a Phyrexian plague, leaving a
personal and practical absence that
the survivors could not repair.

</HistoricalMoment>

<HistoricalEventReference id="hanna-phyrexian-plague" />

For the defenders, the invasion
was no longer an approaching threat.

It had become a fight for the
survival of their world.

## IX. The Rathi Overlay

Rath had been prepared as more
than a distant military outpost.

Its ultimate purpose was revealed
when the artificial plane began
to overlay Dominaria.

The Rathi Overlay brought parts
of Rath and its armies directly
onto the invaded world.

The Stronghold and other features
of the artificial plane became
part of the battlefield.

Phyrexia's armies could now
advance through territory that
had been transformed by the
invasion itself.

The fusion of two planes changed landscapes,
transplanted populations, and allowed Phyrexian
armies to appear across Dominaria without relying
on ordinary routes of invasion.

<HistoricalEventReference id="rathi-overlay" />

The conflict spread across
many regions of Dominaria.

The defenders included soldiers,
mages, ancient peoples, and
figures who had once been enemies.

At the same time, Urza and
a group of planeswalkers
attacked Phyrexia directly.

Their campaign devastated parts
of the enemy plane, but it
did not immediately end the war.

Yawgmoth remained the
central threat.

## X. The Last Confrontation

As the war approached its
conclusion, the struggle
became increasingly desperate.

Even Urza's resolve was tested
by the power and artifice
of Phyrexia.

Gerrard, meanwhile, faced
the temptation of recovering
what the war had taken
from him.

Both were drawn into
Yawgmoth's influence.

A confrontation between
Gerrard and Urza ended with
Urza's decapitation, though
the planeswalker did not
immediately die.

The final stages of the
conflict brought the enemy
Urza had pursued for
millennia onto Dominaria
itself.

Yawgmoth manifested as a
vast, deadly presence,
spreading destruction
across the world.

Conventional resistance
was no longer sufficient.

Even enormous reserves
of magical power failed
to destroy him completely.

The Legacy remained the
defenders' final hope.

## XI. The Legacy Weapon

The scattered pieces of
Urza's plan were finally
brought together.

The Weatherlight, Karn,
Gerrard, and the artifacts
of the Legacy all had a
role in the final weapon.

The two ancient powerstones
that had once divided Urza
and Mishra were also essential.

<HistoricalMoment card={frontmatter.cards[4]}>

The Mightstone and Weakstone
had passed from the ancient
Thran world into the hands
of the brothers.

They had survived the
Brothers' War and become
part of Urza himself.

In the final confrontation,
those stones helped complete
the Legacy through Karn.

The resulting power destroyed
Yawgmoth and brought the
invasion to its end.

</HistoricalMoment>

The victory demanded the
lives of Gerrard and Urza.

The Weatherlight was also
consumed by the culmination
of the Legacy's purpose.

The central struggle that
had linked the Thran,
the Brothers' War, and
Urza's long campaign
against Phyrexia was over.

But its consequences
would continue.

## XII. Aftermath and a New Beginning

Dominaria survived,
but the invasion left
devastated lands and
countless dead.

The victory did not
restore the world to
what it had been.

For the surviving members
of the Weatherlight crew,
the end of the war meant
the loss of companions
and the conclusion of
a journey that had
defined their lives.

Karn survived the final
release of the Legacy
and became a planeswalker.

The silver golem,
once created as part
of Urza's plan,
would go on to shape
later events in
the Multiverse.

The apparent defeat
of Phyrexia also
concealed a danger.

Phyrexian corruption
would eventually
reemerge elsewhere,
setting the stage for
conflicts far beyond
the original invasion.

## XIII. How Magic Told This Story

The Weatherlight Saga
marked a major shift
in how Magic told
its fictional history.

Earlier expansions
often introduced
lore through individual
cards and short
pieces of flavor text.

The Weatherlight storyline
introduced an ongoing
cast whose adventures
could continue across
multiple sets.

_Weatherlight_ (1997)
helped launch this
continuous narrative.

_Tempest_, _Stronghold_,
and _Exodus_ developed
the crew's adventures
on Rath.

The _Urza's Saga_ block,
released from 1998
to 1999, looked backward
to explore preparations
that preceded the main
Weatherlight story.

_Mercadian Masques_
(1999) moved the crew
to another plane.

Finally, _Invasion_
(2000), _Planeshift_
(2001), and _Apocalypse_
(2001) portrayed the
Phyrexian invasion
and its conclusion.

Novels and other fiction
expanded these events
beyond what could be
shown on cards alone.

Later releases also
returned to the saga's
characters.

For example, _Modern
Horizons_ (2019)
portrayed Sisay as
a legendary creature.

The result is a history
told through different
media, across different
years, and sometimes
in an order very
different from the
fictional sequence.

Two selected cards help illustrate the personal
cost of the voyage: _Hanna, Ship's Navigator_,
first printed in _Invasion_ (2000), and _Unmask_,
printed in _Mercadian Masques_ (1999). The latter
actually depicts Volrath shedding his Takara disguise,
connecting an important plot revelation to a card's art.

A later expansion, _Planar Chaos_ (2007), portrayed a
counterfactual path in which Mirri, not Crovax, killed
Selenia and inherited the vampiric curse. These cards
illustrate an alternate history, not the primary
Weatherlight chronology described in this chapter.

The Weatherlight Saga
is therefore both
a defining period
in Dominaria's history
and an important
turning point in
Magic's history
as a storytelling game.
