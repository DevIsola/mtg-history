# Phase 15 decorative landscape

Tool: built-in imagegen. Final project asset: src/assets/phase15-atmosphere.jpg. Used only as atmospheric site illustration, never as historical evidence.

## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a decorative, wide website banner landscape for an editorial fantasy-history website. Create an original atmospheric painted matte landscape, very wide 3:1 panoramic composition: ivory stone spires and bridges on high cliffs, sunlit waterfalls into mist, layered mountains and distant floating architectural islands under a warm golden clouded sky. One tiny anonymous cloaked traveler looks toward the city from the far right foreground. The left third is a quiet, dark blue-black forest and rock silhouette with very little detail so HTML text can sit beside it. Concentrate the golden city and bright detail in the right two thirds, with elegant restrained amber, warm ivory, muted teal and charcoal colors. Fine painterly detail, sophisticated book-cover illustration, luminous distance, natural composition. No text, letters, logos, UI, frames, card borders or watermarks. No named characters, identifiable Magic card composition or assertions about a real historical/canonical place. This is noncanonical decorative atmosphere, not a reconstruction or an artwork reference.
