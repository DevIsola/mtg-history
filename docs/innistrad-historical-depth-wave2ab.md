# Wave 2AB - Innistrad historical depth

## Scope

Five source-backed additions to the Innistrad portion of the existing Stories Across the Planes chapter: Sorin's motive for creating Avacyn; Thraben, angelic flights, and practical protections; the Skirsdag's reach; the Helvault's moonsilver origin and stages of use; and the cursemute's limits.

- A Planeswalker's Guide to Innistrad: Introduction (2011): https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/planeswalkers-guide-innistrad-introduction-2011-08-24
- A Planeswalker's Guide to Innistrad: Gavony and Humans (2011): https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/planeswalkers-guide-innistrad-gavony-and-humans-2011-09-28
- From The Vault: Lore (2016 retrospective): https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/vault-lore-2016-08-01
- I Am Avacyn (2016 retrospective): https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/i-am-avacyn-2016-05-18
- Planeswalker's Guide to Avacyn Restored: Part 2 (2012): https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/planeswalkers-guide-to-avacyn-restored-part-2
- The Lunarch Inquisition (2016 retrospective): https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/lunarch-inquisition-2016-05-04

## Editorial safeguards

- No new events, artwork moments, or printing IDs.
- Eleven existing printing IDs, eleven historical event references, and eleven chapter moments remain unchanged.
- Four official sources are added to the existing bibliography, bringing it from fourteen to eighteen.
- Sorin's intent, the church's beliefs, and Avacyn's protection are distinct.
- The Helvault's creation, Griselbrand's imprisonment, and Thalia's coerced breaking of the artifact are distinct.
- The cursemute did not remove every curse or permanently heal Garruk.
- Avacyn's later 2016 corruption/unmaking is not part of the original 2011-2012 arc.
- Ravnica, Zendikar, and the cross-planar conclusion are unchanged outside the four new bibliography records.
