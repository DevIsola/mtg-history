## Editorial safeguards

No new chapter, card printings, character records, or event IDs.

The political interpretation of Dimir's position within the Guildpact is identified as editorial analysis. The original paradox, Project Kraj, Agyrem/Parhelion, and Teysa's accord retain separate causes.

Agyrem's relationship to temporal damage on Dominaria is not treated as identical to the Ravnican guild conspiracy.
