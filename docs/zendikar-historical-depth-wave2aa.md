# Wave 2AA - Zendikar historical depth

## Scope

Five focused additions within the Zendikar arc of the existing Stories Across the Planes chapter:

1. The original 2009 Roil setting.
2. Forty years of hedron construction and the three-planeswalker lock.
3. An earlier near-escape.
4. The distinct Eye, brood, and titan-release events.
5. The 2015-2016 Sea Gate campaign as later history.

## Official sources

- A Planeswalker's Guide to Zendikar (2009): https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/a-planeswalkers-guide-to-zendikar
- The Lithomancer (original story 2014, republished 2024): https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/the-lithomancer
- The Battle of Fort Keff (original story 2010, republished 2024): https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/the-battle-of-fort-keff
- Battle for Zendikar Story Summary (2015): https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/battle-zendikar-story-summary-2015-12-23

## Editorial safeguards

- No new historical event records or card references.
- Eleven original printing UUIDs, eleven illustrations, and eleven event references remain exact.
- The original 2009 guide did not reveal the hedrons' actual purpose.
- The Roil is not equated with a titan attack in every instance.
- Nahiri's earlier restoration, the Eye activation, Nissa's release, and the later battles remain distinct.
- No unverified exact in-world calendar year is introduced.
- One contemporary source is added to the bibliography.
- Ravnica and Innistrad narrative bodies remain unchanged.
