# Wave 2W — Weatherlight: choice within the Legacy

## Editorial purpose

Deepen the canonical Weatherlight chapter with the decisions of Sisay, Gerrard, Karn and Hanna, the invasion engineering of Rath, and a clear boundary between the published storyline and its abandoned development outline. This wave adds **no new historical records, artwork, or canonical printings**.

## Canonical evidence and source limits

- [Weather(light) Report](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/weatherlight-report-2007-12-03), Mark Rosewater (2007): an **official design retrospective**, supporting Sisay's selective disclosure, Gerrard's reluctance, the scattered Legacy, and Karn's recovery from the Sliver Queen. It is not a verbatim novel transcript.
- [Face Cards, Part 2](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/face-cards-part-2-2018-12-03), Mark Rosewater (2018): an **official design retrospective**, supporting Hanna's preference for engineering over Barrin's spell instruction and how the crew obtained Ertai. Design decisions and actual published plot must be distinguished.
- [The Known Mutliverse](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/known-mutliverse-2008-03-19), Wizards (2008): official planar overview describing Rath's stolen planar material, flowstone, Stronghold production and later Overlay. It does not date individual civilians' journeys.
- [Dominaria United States of Design, Part 1](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/dominaria-united-states-design-part-1-2022-08-29), Mark Rosewater (2022): explicitly describes the **discarded** Weatherlight outline. The older resistance-leader Ertai, Hourglass Pendant and Phyrexian-ruled future Dominaria are **not canon** and must not be inserted as historical event records.
- The original *Invasion*, *Planeshift* and *Apocalypse* novels remain the chapter's primary published fiction. Do not promote the design retrospectives to a full certification of scenes from those books.

These four exact official URL/title references are already in the Weatherlight bibliography. No new bibliography item is needed.

## Editorial acceptance

1. Read the five new subsections in order: Karn, Sisay, Hanna, Rath's engineering, and the discarded outline.
2. Confirm the new account of Karn's Sliver Queen encounter is distinct from the final activation of the Legacy Weapon.
3. Confirm Gerrard's choice to rescue Sisay is not described as blanket approval of Urza's eugenic project.
4. Confirm the Rathi Overlay occurs **after** the crew's initial visit to Rath, not during it.
5. Confirm the abandoned outline is presented as out-of-world design history, not a canonical timeline or the alternate history of *Planar Chaos*.
6. Manually inspect all **22 unchanged exact printing IDs** and contextual card interpretations on desktop and mobile. No new card is curated.
7. Run `node --test scripts/weatherlight-depth-wave2w.test.mjs` followed by `pnpm run verify && pnpm exec playwright test`.
