## Canonical correction: Project Kraj

The existing event compresses the aftermath of Momir Vig's research. The official historical retrospective distinguishes three developments: Agrus Kos kills Momir Vig, Project Kraj runs berserk, and Novijen is destroyed. The third narrative paragraph now states these separately and avoids implying that Teysa personally delivered a final blow to Kraj. This qualification preserves the existing broader chapter interpretation while preventing participant attribution from becoming more precise than the official source permits.

## Methodological limits and source hierarchy

1. Official **story fiction** can establish narrated actions and their local order. Retrospective lore accounts summarize earlier fiction and must be labeled as such. Product announcements establish publication context but should not be used to infer unseen character decisions.
2. Source claims are deliberately bounded. In particular, the cited Dominaria United story does not verify the complete Ajani–Jaya betrayal narrative, and the Ixalan announcement does not prove each stage of Kellan's search.
3. All eight entries use **relative chronology**; published release dates are not converted into fictional years. Illustrations are classified as contextual card art, not independent visual evidence of event details.
4. The Wave 2L risk report ranks which events merit future human review. Its scores are neither error probabilities nor certificates of historical accuracy.
