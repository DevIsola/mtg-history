## Decision: split the New Phyrexian Invasion arc

**Status: AGREED INTENT, NOT IMPLEMENTED.** Preserve the earlier two-chapter decision; do not silently treat the existing `march-of-the-machine` chapter as the final architecture. It currently combines the fall and occupation of Mirrodin, preparations for the invasion, the failed Seedcore strike, the interplanar war and the aftermath.

The next structural migration will divide that chronology into:

1. **Rise of New Phyrexia** — Mirrodin's corruption and resistance, praetor rivalries, and the operations leading up to the failed Seedcore strike. Proposed stable ID: `new-phyrexia`.
2. **March of the Machine** — Realmbreaker's interplanar invasion, resistance across planes, Wrenn and Zhalfir, and Elesh Norn's defeat. Retain ID `march-of-the-machine` for the invasion rather than silently changing existing URLs.

The migration is a **separate, explicitly reviewed future PR**, not part of 14A. Before implementing: record an exact event/printing/source redistribution ledger, migrate `chapterId` references and collection relations, add a redirect strategy for deep links, update ordered chapter navigation/timeline/SEO/`hreflang`, create the eleventh PT-BR companion and update all PT-BR identity references and scope, and recalibrate hard-coded content totals and tests. Omenpaths will move from order 10 to order 11. The present ten-chapter count remains accurate until this migration occurs. Do **not** assert that the split is already published.
